## Selected Work / 代表项目

### [cover-generator](https://github.com/Fmaverick/cover-generator)
Content tooling for generating Xiaohongshu-style visual covers.  
一个面向内容创作者的小红书封面生成工具。

Why it matters: creator tooling is crowded, so the real challenge is not generating an image, but building speed, consistency, and taste into one workflow.  
为什么它有意思：内容工具很多，真正稀缺的不是“生成一张图”，而是把速度、一致性和审美一起封装进工作流。

### [tarot](https://github.com/Fmaverick/tarot)
An interactive TypeScript product experiment blending interface, narrative, and lightweight intelligence.  
一个结合界面体验、叙事感和轻量智能的 TypeScript 产品实验。

Why it matters: I like products that feel usable and alive, not just technically complete.  
为什么它有意思：我偏爱那种“有体验感、像活着一样”的产品，而不只是技术上完成了。

### [boss-agent-cli](https://github.com/Fmaverick/boss-agent-cli)
An AI-agent-first CLI for BOSS 直聘 workflows, including job search, filtering, recruiter workflow support, and resume optimization.  
一个围绕 BOSS 直聘工作流设计的 AI Agent CLI，覆盖职位搜索、福利筛选、招聘者流程支持和简历优化。

Note: this project currently lives as a public fork-based implementation and is part of my exploration of agentic workflow products.  
说明：这个项目目前以公开 fork 的形式存在，是我对 agentic workflow 产品形态的一次探索。

### [wechat-writing-style](https://github.com/Fmaverick/wechat-writing-style)
A writing-style skill for Chinese public-account content creation.  
一个服务于中文公众号写作的风格化技能包。

Why it matters: I care about making tacit writing judgment reusable, not just producing one good article at a time.  
为什么它有意思：我想做的不是偶尔写出一篇好文章，而是把隐性的写作判断沉淀成可复用的能力。
